# aynsdev brand

Calm, precise, approachable. Build useful software that feels considered at every scale.

## Recognition

Use the lowercase `aynsdev/ui` wordmark in Inter semibold. The supplied geometric mark in `docs/assets/logo-dark-mode.svg` is the official compact logo. Preserve its viewBox, proportions, internal clear space and two original fills. Use `logo-dark-mode.svg` on dark surfaces and `logo-light-mode.svg` on light surfaces. Both variants are the original artwork from `Downloads/My Logo`: `logo.svg` for light mode and `logo-dark-mode.svg` for dark mode. Preserve their supplied colours. Switch with the selected app theme; do not add a background tile. Never stretch the mark or add effects.

Our signature is the combination of quiet filled surfaces, pill actions, confident type and exact alignment. Use circular markers for disclosure actions; keep inputs rectangular-rounded. Avoid turning every content group into a card.

Keep the shared library neutral. Product themes own their accent and imagery. Use one accent per view for meaningful action or selection, with explicit hover, pressed and readable foreground tokens. Status colours retain their meanings.

## Composition

Page headers place a short title and optional useful description opposite the primary action; stack on narrow screens. Settings group related fields inside one surface. Data pages keep filters close to the results and numeric columns aligned. Empty states explain what is missing and offer one next step. Errors stay beside the failed action with a recovery path.

Comfortable density: 24px panel padding, 20–24px section gaps. Compact density: 16px panel padding, 12–16px section gaps. Touch controls remain at least 44px. Density changes spacing, not readability.

## Type and voice

Use Inter throughout; keep 500 for controls and short product copy, 600 for titles. Panel titles are 18px; compact card and field-group titles are 16px. Long descriptions may use 400 at 16–18px with generous line height. Use tabular numbers for comparable data.

Say what happened and what the person can do. “Changes saved.” “No projects match this search.” “Could not save. Try again.” Avoid celebration for routine actions and vague errors such as “Something went wrong”.

## Imagery and proof

Prefer real product screens and task-relevant imagery. Empty states use a restrained icon or no illustration. Do not invent decorative mascots or gradients to fill space.

The docs Patterns page is the composition reference. Review changes in light and dark themes, on narrow screens, with long labels, keyboard navigation, loading, empty and error states.
